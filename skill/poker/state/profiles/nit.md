# Archetype: Nit

Tight-passive player; opens only premium hands, rarely 3-bet bluffs.

- **Range tendencies**:
  - Preflop: tight opening (~12-15% UTG, ~18% BTN). Almost no offsuit broadways from EP
  - 3-bet range: JJ+, AQs+ only. No bluff 3-bets
  - 4-bet range: QQ+, AK
  - Calls 3-bets with: 99-TT, AQs, AJs, KQs (rarely wider)

- **Postflop tendencies**:
  - Continuation-bet only when improved (pair+, draw); check-folds air
  - Donk-leads are usually strong (set+ or two pair)
  - Triple-barrels on dry boards = nuts. Multi-street bluffs very rare
  - Thin value-bets rivers with middle pair (they're uncomfortable betting but do it for value)

- **Exploitative adjustments**:
  - Fold more to their 3bet/4bet (assume top of GTO range, not the bluff portion)
  - Cbet flop often when checked to — they give up air
  - Believe their big river bets; hero-call only with boats+
  - Float them in position on dry boards; they'll give up turn
